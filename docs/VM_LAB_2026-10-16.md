## Limits and interpretation

The bottleneck profile was 20 -> 6 -> 20 Mbit/s using tc-tbf with a 400 kB limit. Static CAKE used 18 Mbit/s; the adaptive arms began with a 10 Mbit/s setting and ramped. No-control had no CAKE shaper. Initial rates therefore differ. There was one run per arm, no randomization or repetitions, no variance estimate and no cross traffic.

Throughput is the iperf receive mean over the whole profile. Delay percentiles are raw RTT across the whole run, including startup and transients, computed from replied probes. They are not per-phase queue-delay percentiles and exclude probes with no reply.

LeanBridge had the lowest observed delay here but only 5.56 Mbps versus static CAKE's 10.48 Mbps, about half the throughput. Its rate stayed near 6 Mbit/s after capacity returned to 20. This is a delay-for-throughput tradeoff, not evidence that LeanBridge beats a simply tuned fixed CAKE rate. The static middle-phase behavior is not explained by this session. No cake-autorate comparison, real-world risk guarantee, PASS/KILL verdict or approved protocol threshold follows. The proposed v0.5 protocol is still not adopted.

All four supplied run logs ended with `cleanup verified: True`; final checks reported exit code 0, empty namespaces/state, no bytecode output and eth0 NO-CARRIER. The first arm has a separate cleanup-check paste immediately below it. These are session-specific observations, not proof against all failure modes.

Frozen [simulation findings](FINDINGS.md), [manifest](MANIFEST_v0.3.json) and [simulation results](results_v0.3.json) are unchanged. This record neither overwrites them nor retroactively validates the simulator.
